## 🎯 Prerequisites

Students should already have a solid foundation in Western music theory — intervals, major/minor scales, tone and semitone.

## 🎻 Instrument Focus

This course is designed for violin with standard Western tuning (G–D–A–E). No alternate tuning is used except where explicitly discussed for explanatory purposes.

## 📖 What You'll Learn

- The Arabic 24-quarter-tone system and how it relates to Western tuning
- Half-flat (𝄳) and half-sharp (𝄵) notation
- Neutral intervals (Sikah and Awj)
- Ajnas (melodic cells) - Rast, Bayati, Hijaz, and Nahawand
- Maqamat (full scales) and their construction
- Sayr (melodic movement) and modulation techniques
- Qafla (cadential formulas)
